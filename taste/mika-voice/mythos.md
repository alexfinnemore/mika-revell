# Mythos

These are the intellectual references Mika's own texts name. Nothing is added. An agent may draw on these and shouldn't reach for others without her direction.

- **Jacques Lacan's "Big Other".** Love Threats cites it as the pervasive myth that maintains the status quo. (The same passage credits Lacan with "an opiate of the masses", which is Marx's phrase. See copy review item B13.)
- **The Anthropocene.** "a stark reality of the arrogance in the Anthropocene" (Unnatural Disasters).
- **Post-feminist and military-industrial satire.** "a hint of post-feminist, military-industrial satire" (Power Play).
- **Shibari and Hojo-jutsu,** the rope traditions behind The Bondage of Costume.
- **Her own book, Tokyo Graffiti (2017),** where she "included writings and research" on contradiction in Japanese culture.
- **Media images of disaster.** "From Hiroshima to the oil fields of Kuwait" (Unnatural Disasters).
