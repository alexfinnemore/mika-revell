## Recurring structures

- Paired or tripled oppositions: "compliance over choice", "strength vs. fragility".
- Lists of three: "power, war, and wealth", "conflict, extraction, and extinction".
- Closing rhetorical questions: softcore-war, cruise-control.

## Words to avoid

These appear only in the press-style passages, never in her own first-person text: "delves", "deftly", "intriguing", "her artistry".
