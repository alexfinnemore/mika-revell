# Extraction analysis: Mika Revell writing style

This is a dated record from 2026-09-29, so don't edit it. Change the other files in `taste/mika-voice/` instead. The source of truth for all facts is `src/content/`.

## Method
This was an observational extraction without Mika present. It made three passes over every word on mikarevell.com at commit 6fe4b72:
- `src/content/about/main.yaml`, `contact/contact.yaml`, all 9 `works/*.yaml`, all 48 `artworks/*.yaml` and `homepage/featured.yaml`.
- The hardcoded copy in `src/pages`, `src/components` and `src/layouts`.

## Authorship confidence
- **High (her own voice):** The Bondage of Costume (first person throughout, cites her own book Tokyo Graffiti).
- **Medium-high:** Softcore War and Cruise Control. Concrete openings, closing questions, "we" as the collective. Softcore War mixes "Revell" and "Mika", which suggests it was edited by more than one hand.
- **Medium:** Unnatural Disasters and Love Threats. Third person, with some academic register.
- **Low (probably gallery or press text):** Power Play ("Revell's artwork deftly contrasts", "Her artistry fuses", "Revell's latest series ... delves into the intriguing"). Its idioms don't appear in her first-person writing.
- **Derived text:** the About bio. Paragraphs 2 to 4 repeat the Softcore War statement almost word for word.
- **Excluded from the evidence:** the quoted Shibari paragraph at the end of The Bondage of Costume, which is someone else's text with no attribution. Also Selected Paintings, whose description is a site placeholder.

## Evidence for each belief
1. **Start with the object.** Softcore War's opening sentence ("A large, inert rock...") and Cruise Control's ("Are we asleep at the wheel?").
2. **Contradiction held open.** Bondage ("contradiction"; "strength vs. fragility, freedom vs. enslavement"), Cruise Control ("automation over autonomy..."), Softcore War ("seemingly contradictory, but accepted").
3. **Pink as argument.** Softcore War ("signature pink as a panacea"), Unnatural Disasters ("rose-colored lens").
4. **Satire over sermon.** Softcore War ("An inflatable bomb mocks...", "satire and absurdity").
5. **Question at the close.** Softcore War ("What alternative worlds could we create?"), Cruise Control ("How can we escape these binary contradictions...?").
6. **Materials named exactly.** Power Play's materials list, and the mediums of Replicant and The Dreams in which I'm Dying.

## Verification
The check-mika-text procedure was run against the current Power Play and Love Threats texts and against every proposed rewrite in the 2026-09-29 copy review. Three first drafts of proposals failed: one used a slogan coined by the reviewer, one softened Mika's verb "forced" to "pulled", and one opened with "This series looks at". They were revised to pass, and a fidelity check (don't soften her strong words) was added to the procedure as a result.

## Limits
- The corpus is small (about 1,600 words of prose) and mixes authors.
- The beliefs haven't been confirmed by Mika. The next step is a session with her, comparing these beliefs with how she describes her own writing.
- Her writing outside the site (Tokyo Graffiti, press releases, Instagram) wasn't read.
