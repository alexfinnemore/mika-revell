# Review with Mika, 2026-10-01

Mika and Alex answered questions about the voice guide in a Claude Code session. This is a dated record, so don't edit it; the other files in this folder carry the changes.

- **Person.** Third person, by first name: "Mika", not "Revell". The bio opens with "Mika Revell" once. The site's texts were switched to "Mika" the same day.
- **Shape.** Statements open on the object or a question, as the guide said. Endings are free: a closing question is one option, not a rule.
- **Pink.** It seduces and disarms at the same time, and that contradiction is the point.
- **Words.** Mika never wants "delves" or "deftly". "Explores", "interrogate" and "powerful" are fine with her.
- **Her one-line description** is the bio's: "Her work sets symbols of power against objects of desire, contrasting the ominous and the alluring."
- **Writing pages.** First or third person, decided per piece.

Facts confirmed by Alex the same day, recorded in `src/content/`: born 1985 in Sydney, Chinese-American; the Art in Nature Award and RFP Award; residencies in Riyadh, Tokyo, Los Angeles and Tulum, Mexico; "Passion" and "China Town Nights" at the Architecture + Design Museum, Los Angeles, 2020; mediums for Disarmed and Under the Skin; and the corrected matching of SOFTCORE WAR images to their titles.
